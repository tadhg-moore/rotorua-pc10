General Lake Model (GLM 3.x) setup and simulation outputs

Author

Tadhg Moore

Published

29 May 2026

Overview

This page documents the General Lake Model (GLM 3.3.3) configuration used for simulating the lake’s thermal dynamics, water balance, and nutrient cycling. The model is coupled to the Aquatic EcoDynamics (AED) library for biogeochemical processes.

The AEME package was used to retrieve and format lake characteristics, boundary conditions, and monitoring data for model configuration and validation. The glm3.nml and aed.nml files define the physical and biogeochemical model parameters, respectively, while CSV files provide time series of inflows, meteorological forcing, and phytoplankton parameters.

Note

All simulation configuration files, scripts, and outputs documented here correspond to the baseline run (run_id: aeme_base).

TipSchematic notes
  • Inflows: Four principal inflows are configured (Hamurana, Ohau, Waiowhiro, Waiteti streams). Loads are derived from monitored flow and water quality data (BoPRC).
  • Outflow: The Ohau Channel is the sole surface outflow; weir crest elevation set at 279.38 m a.s.l.
  • Meteorology: Half-hourly data from the Rotorua Airport NIWA station (agent 89894), gap-filled using ERA5 reanalysis where needed.
  • WWTP: Treated effluent from Rotorua WWTP is included as a point-source inflow.
  • AED modules active: NIT (nitrogen), PHO (phosphorus), OGM (organic matter), PHY (phytoplankton).

Known issues

WarningActive issues with current simulation

The following issues have been identified and are under investigation. See the issue tracker for details.

Issue 1 — Late-summer TP over-prediction

The model systematically over-predicts surface TP concentrations during February–April by approximately 8–12 mg/m³. This is likely linked to underestimated internal P loading parameterisation under low-oxygen bottom waters. Possible remedies being explored:

  • Tighten Fsed_frp seasonal dependency on oxygen (see aed_sedflux.nml).
  • Implement dynamic sediment release model (AED sed_model = 1).

Priority: High | Assigned to: J. Smith

Issue 2 — Cyanobacteria bloom timing offset

Simulated Anabaena/Aphanizomenon blooms (phytoplankton Group 1) peak ~3 weeks early relative to monitoring observations. Review of T_opt, KPO4, and buoyancy regulation parameters for Group 1 is underway.

Priority: Medium | Assigned to: K. Jones

Issue 3 — Wind sheltering in northerly fetch

The current morphometry configuration does not account for wind sheltering by the Rotorua township and surrounding hills on the north shore. Adding a directional fetch_sw = .true. configuration and wind speed correction factor is planned.

Priority: Low

Issue 4 — ERA5 longwave gap-fill artefacts

Discontinuities in the gap-filled longwave radiation series (ERA5 substituted for missing station data periods) are causing transient surface mixing events. A smoothed blending approach is being implemented.

Priority: Medium | Assigned to: R. Patel

## Overview {#overview}

This page documents the **General Lake Model (GLM `r glm_version`)** configuration used for simulating the lake's thermal dynamics, water balance, and nutrient cycling. The model is coupled to the **Aquatic EcoDynamics (AED)** library for biogeochemical processes.

The [AEME](https://limnotrack.com/AEME/) package was used to retrieve and format lake characteristics, boundary conditions, and monitoring data for model configuration and validation. The `glm3.nml` and `aed.nml` files define the physical and biogeochemical model parameters, respectively, while CSV files provide time series of inflows, meteorological forcing, and phytoplankton parameters.

::: {.callout-note}
All simulation configuration files, scripts, and outputs documented here correspond to the
**baseline run** (`run_id: aeme_base`). 
:::

## Model schematic {#schematic}

The schematic below shows the overall GLM–AED model configuration for Lake Rotorua,
including the principal inflows, outflow, atmospheric forcing, and the biogeochemical
modules activated in this simulation.


::: {.callout-tip collapse="true"}
## Schematic notes

- **Inflows**: Four principal inflows are configured (Hamurana, Ohau, Waiowhiro, Waiteti streams).
  Loads are derived from monitored flow and water quality data (BoPRC).
- **Outflow**: The Ohau Channel is the sole surface outflow; weir crest elevation set at 279.38 m a.s.l.
- **Meteorology**: Half-hourly data from the Rotorua Airport NIWA station (agent 89894), gap-filled
  using ERA5 reanalysis where needed.
- **WWTP**: Treated effluent from Rotorua WWTP is included as a point-source inflow.
- **AED modules active**: NIT (nitrogen), PHO (phosphorus), OGM (organic matter), PHY (phytoplankton).
:::
